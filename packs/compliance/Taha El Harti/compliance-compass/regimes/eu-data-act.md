## Does this apply to you? (triggers)
- Applicable since 12 Sep 2025. Two founder-relevant limbs:
  1. **Cloud switching (Ch VI) — hits SaaS directly.** Providers of "data processing services" — explicitly including SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, edge — serving EU customers, wherever the provider is located.
  2. **Connected products / IoT data sharing** — only if you ship hardware/connected devices.
- No EU customers and no connected hardware → NOT APPLICABLE; re-check when EU sales start.

## Who can punish you, and how
- National authorities (enforcement infrastructure still ramping), but the immediate bite is contractual: clauses contradicting the switching rules are unenforceable now. Customers can invoke this. (Bucket assigned per founder in the report.)

## Penalties & enforcement reality
- Penalties set by member states (GDPR-level caps for some violations); verified 2026-07-28.
- **Honesty statement:** enforcement infrastructure is still ramping — no small-company enforcement record in the source research — but the contract-validity effect is immediate, and most startup MSAs haven't been updated. That's the actual gap.

## Gap-check questions
1. Are you SaaS (or PaaS/IaaS) with EU customers?
2. Does your customer contract let a customer leave with max 2 months' notice, with the switch completed within 30 days?
3. Can customers export their data in a machine-readable format?
4. Do you charge switching/egress fees anywhere in your pricing?
5. Do you ship any connected hardware or devices?

## First steps
1. Read your own MSA against the switching rules: notice ≤2 months, switch within 30 days (extendable to 7 months only if technically unfeasible), machine-readable export, no lock-in clauses. (hours)
2. Remove or sunset switching fees — reduced now, prohibited from 12 Jan 2027. (hours–days)
3. If you ship connected hardware, treat the IoT data-sharing limb as a separate work item. (assessment: hours)
