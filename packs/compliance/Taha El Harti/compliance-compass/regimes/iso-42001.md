## Does this apply to you? (triggers)

**Watch item, not a to-do.** Demand is real but early-stage and segment-specific
(verified 2026-07-28): enterprise AI-vendor questionnaires now carry AI-governance
sections, and ISO 42001 appears as a named ask from financial services, healthcare, and
government buyers of AI products. **Almost no startup needs the certificate yet unless a
named buyer demands it.** Not selling an AI product to regulated enterprises → NOT
APPLICABLE (re-check yearly, or when an AI-governance questionnaire section arrives).

Adoption reality: still a small club dominated by large vendors — Anthropic certified
Jan 2025, Snowflake Jun 2025, ServiceNow Dec 2025; BCG (Jan 2026) said it was among the
first ~100 certified organizations globally.

## Who can punish you, and how

No regulator; buyers only, via questionnaires and (rarely, so far) contract terms.
**Bucket note:** market-expected today; migrates to contract-mandated the moment an MSA
names it — the report assigns the bucket per founder.

## Penalties & enforcement reality

Commercial only. Honesty statement: in 2026 the practical cost of not having it is an
awkward questionnaire answer, not a lost deal — "aligned with NIST AI RMF" (a voluntary,
non-certifiable US framework for managing AI risk) is currently an acceptable answer
where no certificate exists, and RMF work feeds a later ISO 42001 effort.

## Cost baselines (2026, verified 2026-07-28; certifier/consultancy figures — skew high)

- SMB certification audits ~$7.5k–$25k; realistic small-company all-in $20k–$85k+.
- Timeline 4–12 months; ~30–50% savings if an ISO 27001 management system already exists.

## Gap-check questions

1. Do you sell an AI product (or AI features) to banks, insurers, healthcare, or
   government?
2. Has any buyer's questionnaire included an AI-governance section, or named ISO 42001?
3. Do you have any written statement of how you manage AI risk (even one page)?
4. Do you already hold ISO 27001 (cuts a future 42001 effort by roughly a third to half)?

## First steps

1. Track, don't buy: note it as a watch item unless a named buyer demands the
   certificate. 2. Keep a lightweight written AI-risk statement aligned to NIST AI RMF as
   the questionnaire answer. 3. Re-evaluate if it appears in standard SIG/CAIQ releases
   or a major buyer segment makes it a gate.
