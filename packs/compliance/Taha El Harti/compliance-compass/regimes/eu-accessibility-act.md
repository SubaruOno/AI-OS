## Does this apply to you? (triggers)
- In force for services since 28 Jun 2025. Covers consumer-facing digital services: e-commerce (any site/app selling to EU consumers — broad), banking, transport ticketing, telecoms, e-books, audiovisual media access.
- B2C SaaS, or any product with an EU consumer checkout: **yes** — the e-commerce limb captures the purchase funnel at minimum; consumer-facing service content likely too.
- Pure B2B SaaS: generally outside the consumer-services scope — but flows in via procurement (public sector, large enterprises) and via the e-commerce limb if selling online to prosumers.
- **Microenterprise exemption (services): <10 employees AND <€2M turnover** → NOT APPLICABLE while both hold; lost immediately on crossing either threshold — re-check at each hire/revenue milestone.
- Technical benchmark in practice: EN 301 549 / WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Who can punish you, and how
- National market-surveillance authorities: inspections, formal information requests (including to non-EU companies), warning letters, fines, orders to withdraw the service. Civil-society lawsuits are part of the 2026 pattern. (Bucket assigned per founder in the report.)

## Penalties & enforcement reality
- Penalties are national — fines to ~€500k+ in some states, plus service-withdrawal orders (verified 2026-07-28).
- **Honesty statement:** enforcement is escalating but not yet GDPR-scale — the 2026 pattern is inspections, information requests, warning letters, and lawsuits rather than headline fines (verified 2026-07-28).

## Gap-check questions
1. Do EU consumers use or buy your product (vs businesses only)?
2. Is there a checkout or purchase flow consumers go through online?
3. Are you at or past 10 employees or €2M turnover?
4. Has anyone ever run an accessibility audit (WCAG 2.1 AA) on your product?
5. Do you publish an accessibility statement?

## First steps
1. Run the exemption test: headcount and turnover, in writing. (hours)
2. If in scope: audit the consumer-facing funnel (checkout first) against WCAG 2.1 AA. (days)
3. Publish an accessibility statement; remediate highest-traffic pages first. (days)

## Cost baselines
Vendor-sourced — verify before quoting (as of 2026-07-28): audit only, small site — low thousands €; full remediation of a complex app — five figures.
