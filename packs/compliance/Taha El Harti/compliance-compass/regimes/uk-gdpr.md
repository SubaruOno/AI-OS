## Does this apply to you? (triggers)

Fits the GDPR skeleton almost perfectly (see `gdpr-family-pattern.md`); this quick-ref
carries only the UK deltas. Applies if you are established in the UK, or offer
goods/services to or monitor people in the UK. No UK establishment, customers, or
monitoring → NOT APPLICABLE (re-check on UK expansion).

## Who can punish you, and how

The ICO — a real, active regulator — plus private claims. **Bucket note:** this is the
law bucket where it applies; unlike the market frameworks in this library it does not
depend on a contract naming it (though DPAs you sign can add stricter duties on top —
the report assigns buckets per founder).

## DUAA 2025 deltas from EU GDPR (verified 2026-07-28)

Royal assent 19 Jun 2025; majority of provisions in force from 5 Feb 2026:

- New **"recognised legitimate interests"** lawful basis — for defined purposes, no
  balancing test (the case-by-case weighing EU GDPR requires) is needed.
- Relaxed rules for some automated decision-making.
- **PECR e-marketing fines raised to UK GDPR levels** (previously capped at £500k) —
  cookie and marketing enforcement now carries real teeth.
- Mandatory data-subject **complaints procedure** for all controllers since 19 Jun 2026,
  with 30-day acknowledgement — every UK controller needs this now.
- EU adequacy for the UK was renewed/extended through the DUAA process — UK↔EU data flows
  remain free (monitor: adequacy is reviewable).

## Penalties & enforcement reality (verified 2026-07-28)

- Maxima: £17.5m or 4% of global turnover.
- ICO posture — record year: Capita £14m (Oct 2025, largest ever); 23andMe £2.31m (2025);
  Reddit + Imgur ~£14.5m combined (Feb 2026) over children's data.
- Enforcement themes: security failures, children's data, and (newly credible after the
  PECR uplift) marketing/cookies.
- Honesty statement: headline fines target large firms; small-company enforcement is
  complaint-driven — marketing/cookie practices are the newest realistic small-company
  exposure. (Small-company fine examples: not established in source research.)

## Gap-check questions

1. Do you have UK customers, users, or a UK entity?
2. Do you send marketing emails or use tracking cookies for UK users with their prior
   agreement (the rules known as PECR — now carrying GDPR-level fines)?
3. Do you have a published way for individuals to complain to you about their data, with
   an acknowledgement inside 30 days (required since 19 Jun 2026)?
4. If you handle children's data: have you reviewed it recently? (Top ICO theme.)
5. Are your EU-GDPR documents (privacy notice, records) adapted for the UK deltas above?

## First steps

1. Stand up the 30-day complaints procedure — already mandatory. (Hours to a day.)
2. Audit cookie banners and e-marketing consent — the fine ceiling just rose ~35x. (Day.)
3. Map where the new "recognised legitimate interests" basis simplifies your processing
   records. (Hours.)
