# Drop your documents here (optional)

Anything you put in this folder gets read at the start of the interview, and the engine
opens the conversation with what it found. Useful things to drop in:

- Your privacy policy / terms of service
- A security questionnaire a customer sent you
- A DPA (data processing agreement) or BAA someone asked you to sign
- Your cookie banner config, a pentest report, an old audit — anything compliance-shaped

A handful of key documents works best. You don't need any of them — an empty folder is
completely fine.
