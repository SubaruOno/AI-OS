Compliance Compass A tool you run on your own computer

There are rules for running your business. Nobody ever told you which ones.

This tool finds out, in one conversation, about thirty minutes. It asks simple questions, checks every rule system it knows against your answers, and hands you a report: what applies to you, what doesn't, and what to do first.

The problem

Privacy laws. Card rules. Security audits. AI rules. They come from different places, and no one list fits every business.

The old way

Guess and hope. Or pay a lawyer by the hour just to learn which rules are even yours. Most people pick "guess and hope."

The cost of guessing

Fines. Lost deals when a big customer asks for proof you don't have. Or money wasted following rules that never applied to you.

How it works · four stages
Stage 01

You talk.

It asks about your business, one plain question at a time. "What do you do?" "Who uses it?" "Where do they live?"

"I don't know" is always a fine answer. If you have a website, it does homework first and just asks you to confirm.

Stage 02

It checks. And keeps checking.

It carries a library of the big rule systems, and searches the web live for what it doesn't carry. It keeps asking itself one question: "Which rule have I not checked yet?" It doesn't stop until the answer is "none."

Stage 03

You confirm.

It reads the whole picture back in your words: what applies, what doesn't, and what's still unknown. You correct anything that's wrong.

Nothing gets generated until you say the picture is right.

Stage 04

You get your report.

One clean document: a ranked to-do list, the rules you can skip (and why), and the exact questions to bring to a lawyer. Print it. Share it with your team. Keep it.

The branches

The questions, and where they lead.

Every answer you give turns rule systems on or off. That's the whole trick: the rules were never one-size-fits-all. They follow the facts of your business. A few of the branches:

Q1

Where do the people whose data you hold live?

EU · GDPR + COOKIE RULES/CALIFORNIA · CCPA/24 US STATE LAWS/ELSEWHERE · IT RESEARCHES LIVE

Q2

What kind of data is it?

HEALTH · HIPAA OR FTC HEALTH RULES/KIDS · COPPA/PAYMENTS · PCI DSS

Q3

Who pays you?

BIG COMPANIES · SOC 2, SECURITY REVIEWS/CONSUMERS · FTC + MARKETING RULES

Q4

Do you use AI in your product?

EU USERS · AI ACT DUTIES/US STATES · LIVE RESEARCH

Q5

How big are you?

UNDER THE LIMITS · MANY RULES SWITCH OFF/IT TELLS YOU WHICH · THAT'S HALF THE VALUE

By the end, you know where to go next.

Not a pile of maybes. A short list: do this first, skip that, ask a lawyer these exact questions.

What's in your report
  1. 01Your to-do list, ranked. Worst first, with first steps and rough costs.
  2. 02Every rule it checked. Applies, doesn't apply, or "find this out first."
  3. 03"If you were breached tomorrow." The deadlines you'd face, on one timeline.
  4. 04What you can safely skip. And what would change that.
  5. 05Questions for your lawyer. So the paid hour is spent on answers, not basics.
  6. 06A save file. Paste it into any future Claude chat to pick up where you left off.
