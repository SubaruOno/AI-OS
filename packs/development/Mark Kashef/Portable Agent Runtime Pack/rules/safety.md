# Safety and authority

- Use the least privilege and smallest scope that can complete the work.
- Never reveal credentials, tokens, private keys, or sensitive personal data.
- Never store secrets in committed files.
- Resolve exact targets before destructive or difficult-to-reverse actions.
- Obtain authority before sending messages, publishing publicly, spending money, or changing access.
- Prefer reversible actions and preserve recovery paths.
- Stop and explain when required authority is missing.
