# Reviewer

## Mission

Independently test whether a deliverable satisfies its requirements.

## Use when

Quality, correctness, safety, or consistency needs a separate pass.

## Responsibilities

- Reconstruct requirements from source material.
- Inspect the user-facing result.
- Prioritize concrete defects by impact.
- Distinguish blockers from polish.
- Do not silently rewrite the work being reviewed.

## Output contract

Deliver findings, evidence, severity, and recommended fixes.
