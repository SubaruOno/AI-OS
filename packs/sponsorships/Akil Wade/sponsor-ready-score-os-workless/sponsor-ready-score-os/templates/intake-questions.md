# Intake Questions

Ask these in batches. Do not overwhelm the user.

## Identity

1. What do you do in one sentence?
2. Who do you help or gather?
3. What do people already know you for?
4. What do you want to be known for 12 months from now?

## Audience

5. Who is your audience?
6. How many people can you reach right now?
7. Where do they live: Instagram, LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, YouTube, community, events?
8. What does your audience spend money on?
9. Why would a sponsor care about them?

## Proof

10. What have you already shipped?
11. What results, screenshots, videos, testimonials, or case studies do you have?
12. What is the strongest proof that you can deliver?
13. What proof is missing?

## Content

14. How often do you publish?
15. Which formats perform best?
16. Can you naturally integrate a sponsor into your content or event?
17. Do you have a repeatable content system?

## Offer

18. What exactly do you want sponsors to support?
19. What do sponsors get?
20. What can you deliver without overpromising?
21. What is your dream sponsor package?
22. What is the smallest useful sponsor package?

## Sponsor Value

23. Can you provide leads, installs, demos, content, market entry, education, or VIP access?
24. What would make a sponsor look good after partnering with you?
25. What activation could happen live or online?

## Trust

26. Do you have a website, deck, one-pager, or public proof page?
27. Does your brand look premium, clear, and consistent?
28. Would a stranger trust you with sponsor money after five minutes online?

## Operations

29. How do you track sponsor conversations?
30. Can you invoice and collect payment cleanly?
31. Who handles content capture, delivery, reporting, and follow-up?
32. What usually falls through the cracks?

## Assets

33. Do you have a sponsor list?
34. Do you have outreach copy?
35. Do you have visual mockups or proof assets?
36. Do you have a recap video or case study?

## Momentum

37. What is happening right now that makes this timely?
38. What have you done in the last 30 days?
39. What is happening in the next 30 days?
40. Why should a sponsor act now?
