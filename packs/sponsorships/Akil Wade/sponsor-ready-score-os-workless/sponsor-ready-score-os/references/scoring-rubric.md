# Scoring Rubric

Each category is scored from 0 to 100.

## 1. Positioning

Measures whether people quickly understand who the user is, what they do, who they serve, and why it matters.

- 0-20: confusing, generic, no clear lane
- 21-40: some direction but hard to explain
- 41-60: clear niche but weak differentiation
- 61-80: strong category and story
- 81-100: instantly clear, memorable, sponsor-friendly positioning

## 2. Audience

Measures whether the user has a defined, reachable audience sponsors care about.

- 0-20: no audience or undefined audience
- 21-40: small or scattered audience
- 41-60: clear audience but limited proof of engagement
- 61-80: engaged audience with useful sponsor fit
- 81-100: high-value audience with clear buying power or cultural influence

## 3. Proof

Measures evidence that the user can create outcomes.

Proof can include past events, revenue, content performance, testimonials, case studies, attendee photos, community screenshots, press, client results, or shipped projects.

- 0-20: no visible proof
- 21-40: private proof but little public evidence
- 41-60: some visible proof
- 61-80: strong proof across multiple formats
- 81-100: undeniable proof that makes sponsors trust delivery

## 4. Content Engine

Measures whether the user can consistently create attention and assets.

- 0-20: rarely posts or publishes
- 21-40: inconsistent content
- 41-60: posts but no clear system
- 61-80: consistent content with repeatable formats
- 81-100: strong content machine with clear distribution and sponsor integration ability

## 5. Offer Clarity

Measures whether the user knows exactly what they are selling or inviting sponsors into.

- 0-20: no clear offer
- 21-40: vague package
- 41-60: basic offer with missing details
- 61-80: clear offer and deliverables
- 81-100: compelling offer tied to measurable sponsor outcomes

## 6. Sponsor Value

Measures whether the sponsor gets something beyond visibility.

Look for leads, installs, demos, content, market entry, trust, education, VIP access, data, recruiting, or community goodwill.

- 0-20: logo placement only
- 21-40: mentions and visibility
- 41-60: some activation ideas
- 61-80: strong sponsor outcome
- 81-100: custom sponsor value that feels hard to replace

## 7. Brand Trust

Measures whether the user looks legitimate.

Includes website, deck, visuals, email presence, payment process, social proof, brand consistency, and professionalism.

- 0-20: looks untrustworthy or unfinished
- 21-40: messy but real
- 41-60: decent but inconsistent
- 61-80: professional and credible
- 81-100: premium, high-trust, sponsor-ready brand presence

## 8. Operations

Measures ability to deliver after a sponsor says yes.

Includes timelines, contracts, invoices, content capture, team roles, reporting, file delivery, follow-up, and project management.

- 0-20: no operating system
- 21-40: reactive and manual
- 41-60: basic task management
- 61-80: organized enough to deliver
- 81-100: sponsor operations feel polished and reliable

## 9. Outreach Assets

Measures whether the user has the assets needed to approach sponsors.

Assets include sponsor list, sponsor deck, one-pager, recap video, proof images, email scripts, activation menu, and tracker.

- 0-20: no assets
- 21-40: rough notes only
- 41-60: basic deck or pitch
- 61-80: good outreach kit
- 81-100: premium outreach system with custom sponsor proof assets

## 10. Momentum

Measures whether the user has current activity that makes the opportunity feel alive.

Examples: recent event, growing RSVPs, active community, weekly content, confirmed speakers, press, partnerships, new offer, product launch.

- 0-20: stale or inactive
- 21-40: some recent movement
- 41-60: active but not loud
- 61-80: clear momentum
- 81-100: strong public momentum sponsors can feel
