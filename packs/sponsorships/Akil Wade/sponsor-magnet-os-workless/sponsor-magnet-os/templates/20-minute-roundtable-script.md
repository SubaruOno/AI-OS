# 20-Minute Roundtable Script

## 0:00-2:00 Open

Most people pitch sponsors wrong.

They say, "We have an audience. Put your logo here."

The better move is to ask: what does this sponsor actually need right now?

Sometimes it is leads. Sometimes it is product adoption. Sometimes it is content. Sometimes it is a market entry moment. If you can figure that out, you stop selling sponsorship and start designing an outcome.

## 2:00-5:00 The Core Framework

Every sponsor falls into one of five motive buckets:

- Pipeline
- Proof
- Market entry
- Brand heat
- Education

Once you know the bucket, you can build the activation.

## 5:00-8:00 Show The Folder

This folder gives you:

- a sponsorship skill
- sponsor research prompts
- outreach templates
- proof asset prompts
- activation menu
- sponsor tracker
- call notes template

Drop the folder into your AI OS and say:

```text
Analyze this sponsorship folder and help me find 30 sponsors for my event. Start by asking me for the event details you need.
```

## 8:00-12:00 The Proof Asset Move

One of the fastest ways to stand out is to show the sponsor what the activation could look like before it exists.

Instead of only sending a PDF, create a visual:

- their product at your event
- their booth or station
- attendees using the product
- a founder dinner
- a stage moment
- a recap film concept

You are not pretending it already happened. You are helping them picture the opportunity.

## 12:00-18:00 Around The Table

Ask each person:

- What event are you running?
- Who is in the room?
- What kind of sponsor do you want?
- What would that sponsor want from your audience?

Then give them one activation idea and one sponsor category.

## 18:00-20:00 Close

The takeaway:

Do not sell logo placement.

Sell the sponsor a moment, an outcome, and proof they can use after the event.
