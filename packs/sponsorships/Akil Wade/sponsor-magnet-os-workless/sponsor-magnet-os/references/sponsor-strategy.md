# Sponsor Strategy

## The Shift

Weak sponsorship pitch:

> We have an event with 300 people. You can sponsor it.

Strong sponsorship pitch:

> You are trying to enter this audience, create proof, and start conversations with buyers. We can build you a live moment inside the room that gives you content, leads, product adoption, and trust.

## The Five Sponsor Motives

### 1. Pipeline

They want buyers, meetings, leads, opt-ins, demos, or sales conversations.

Best activation:

- QR opt-in
- VIP dinner
- founder round table
- live diagnostic
- demo station
- private buyer breakfast

### 2. Proof

They want content that makes the product look adopted, loved, and real.

Best activation:

- filmed product install
- live transformation moment
- testimonial booth
- before/after workflow
- founder reaction clips
- recap video

### 3. Market Entry

They want credibility in a new city, country, industry, or community.

Best activation:

- local partner moment
- panel
- community welcome
- country-specific case study
- co-branded event page

### 4. Brand Heat

They want to look culturally relevant.

Best activation:

- premium merch
- photo wall
- creator dinner
- branded lounge
- hosted experience
- high-quality aftermovie

### 5. Education

They need the audience to understand a new behavior.

Best activation:

- workshop
- guided install
- onstage demo
- playbook handout
- use-case clinic

## Sponsor Package Ladder

Use this ladder as a starting point. Adjust based on audience quality, proof, sponsor category, and event production value.

### Support Partner

For smaller brands or first-time sponsor tests.

Includes:

- logo on site
- mention in email
- one simple activation
- 3-5 photos
- recap inclusion

### Activation Partner

For brands that want a real in-room moment.

Includes:

- branded table or station
- QR lead capture
- demo or workshop segment
- social mention
- photo/video capture
- opt-in report

### Presenting Partner

For brands that want to own the room.

Includes:

- naming association
- opening or featured speaking slot
- premium in-room branding
- lead capture
- custom activation
- recap video
- content library
- post-event report

### Category Exclusive Partner

For brands that want no competitors in their lane.

Includes:

- category exclusivity
- all presenting partner assets
- priority speaking moment
- deeper content package
- partner intro support

## Price Anchor Logic

Price based on value, not headcount alone.

Consider:

- Ticket price
- Buyer quality
- International attendance
- Production quality
- Content captured
- Speaker credibility
- Category exclusivity
- Lead capture
- Whether the sponsor can reuse footage in ads

If paid usage or ad rights are included, price higher. If the sponsor gets raw footage, testimonials, or whitelisting, treat that as a separate value line.

## The Sponsor Research Loop

For each target sponsor:

1. Find what they are currently launching.
2. Identify the audience they need.
3. Look for regions or verticals they are trying to enter.
4. Look for content style they already use.
5. Find the person who owns partnerships, field marketing, creator marketing, events, community, or growth.
6. Build an angle that connects their current priority to the event.

## What Makes A Sponsor Say Yes

- The event has a specific audience they already want.
- The activation feels easy to execute.
- The sponsor can picture the final photos/video.
- The ask is clear.
- The organizer looks like they can actually deliver.
- The sponsor sees more than "awareness."
