# Proof Asset Briefs

These prompts help a sponsor see the activation before it exists.

## Premium Event Activation Image

```text
Create a cinematic sponsor activation image for [brand] at [event].

The scene is a premium event room with [audience] gathered around a branded activation station. The sponsor presence is tasteful and integrated, not overpowering. Attendees are interacting with the product, scanning a QR code, talking to the team, and reacting with curiosity.

Show high-quality lighting, realistic event production, clean signage, premium materials, laptops, cameras, and a polished founder/business audience. The image should feel like a luxury private event, not a trade show booth.

Style: cinematic documentary photography, subtle film grain, warm architectural light, premium black and neutral palette, high-trust atmosphere, realistic human interaction, no cheesy flyer design, no fake-looking text.
```

## Sponsor Recap Film Prompt

```text
Create a 9:16 cinematic video concept for [brand] sponsoring [event].

Show fast emotional cuts:
1. Guests arriving
2. Sponsor logo tastefully placed at check-in
3. Attendees scanning a QR code
4. A founder using the sponsor product live
5. A speaker mentioning the sponsor on stage
6. Small group conversations
7. A testimonial moment
8. Premium crowd energy
9. Final hero shot of the room

Feeling: premium, energetic, smart, global, founder-led, high-status, real. Avoid corporate stock footage and generic tech visuals.
```

## Guided Install / Product Adoption Prompt

```text
Create a realistic image of a live guided product install activation at [event].

Attendees sit around tables with laptops open while the host walks them through using [brand/product]. The sponsor's branding appears on a screen and a small table sign. The room feels premium and intimate. People are focused, learning, smiling, and actively using the product.

Style: cinematic documentary event photography, warm natural light, clean luxury venue, realistic diverse founders and operators, premium but not staged, no generic conference look.
```

## Sponsor Dinner Prompt

```text
Create a premium private sponsor dinner image for [brand] at [event].

The scene is an intimate dinner with founders, creators, and operators around a beautiful table. Subtle sponsor branding appears on menus, place cards, and a small screen. The mood is warm, exclusive, high-trust, and conversational.

Style: luxury editorial photography, warm candlelight, black and ivory details, film grain, real human expressions, private members club energy.
```

## Raffle / Giveaway Prompt

```text
Create a cinematic event moment where [brand] hosts a live giveaway on stage.

The screen shows a tasteful branded giveaway visual. Attendees are excited, phones out, and one winner is being called up. The sponsor team stands nearby. The scene should feel energetic, premium, and human.

Style: high-end event photography, dramatic stage light, realistic crowd, clean branding, no cheap promo aesthetic.
```

## Sponsor One-Pager Visual Direction

```text
Design a one-page sponsorship proposal visual for [brand] x [event].

The page should feel premium and simple: strong hero image, clear event facts, audience profile, custom activation concept, content deliverables, and package price. Use luxury editorial layout, large cinematic imagery, clean typography, generous spacing, and no clutter.

Avoid generic sponsorship deck design, rainbow gradients, overused icons, and too much text.
```
