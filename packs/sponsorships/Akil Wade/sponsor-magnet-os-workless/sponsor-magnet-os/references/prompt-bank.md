# Prompt Bank

Use these prompts inside any AI assistant. Replace the bracketed fields.

## Sponsor Target Map

```text
You are my sponsorship strategist.

Event:
- Name: [event name]
- Date/location: [date/location]
- Audience size: [number]
- Audience profile: [who attends]
- Ticket price: [price]
- Event format: [workshop/panel/dinner/retreat/conference]
- Proof: [speakers, past event, RSVP count, content, community]

Build me a sponsor target map with 50 companies.

For each company include:
- sponsor category
- why they fit this audience
- what they likely want right now
- best activation idea
- best person/title to contact
- outreach angle
- suggested package: support, activation, presenting, or category exclusive
- confidence score from 1-5
```

## Campaign-Driven Sponsor Research

```text
Research [company] as a potential sponsor for [event].

Find or infer:
- what product or campaign they are pushing right now
- what audience they are trying to reach
- what market, region, or behavior they may want to grow
- what content style they already use
- what event activation would help them
- what proof asset would make them excited

Then create:
1. A one-sentence sponsorship angle
2. Three activation ideas
3. A short outreach message
4. A follow-up message
5. A suggested price anchor
```

## Sponsor Activation Builder

```text
Design a custom sponsor activation for [brand] at [event].

Brand:
- Product:
- Current campaign:
- Audience they want:
- Likely business goal:

Event:
- Audience:
- Format:
- Venue:
- Content capture available:

Give me:
- activation name
- what happens in the room
- what attendees do
- what the sponsor gets
- what content gets captured
- how leads or opt-ins are collected
- what makes this feel premium
- how to explain it in one paragraph
```

## Proof Asset Prompt Generator

```text
Create a visual prompt for a sponsor proof image.

Sponsor: [brand]
Event: [event]
Venue/location: [venue]
Audience: [audience]
Activation: [activation idea]
Visual feeling: premium, cinematic, realistic, high-trust, not a flyer

Write a prompt for an AI image/video tool that shows what it would feel like if the sponsor activated at the event. The image should help the sponsor emotionally understand the opportunity before signing.
```

## Sponsor Offer Builder

```text
Turn this event into three sponsor packages.

Event details:
[paste event details]

Create:
1. Low package
2. Mid package
3. Premium package

For each include:
- package name
- who it is for
- deliverables
- activation
- content assets
- reporting
- what is not included
- suggested price
- upgrade path

Avoid generic logo-placement language. Make each package outcome-driven.
```

## Reply Analyzer

```text
Analyze this sponsor reply and tell me the move.

Sponsor reply:
[paste reply]

Tell me:
- what they are really asking
- how warm they are
- whether budget is likely there
- what objection is hidden
- what to ask next
- whether to send a proposal, schedule a call, or follow up later
- a short reply I can send
```
