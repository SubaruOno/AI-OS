## The four skills

**`ig-icp-research`**: Day 1, once
Scans Instagram and TikTok for what your buyer actually watches. Returns their pains in their own words, the accounts to engage with, and reels that already sold your kind of offer to your kind of buyer.

Ends with a verdict: **green, amber or red.** If it says red, your buyer is not reachable this way and it tells you what to do instead. That is a feature. It saves you 30 days.

**`ig-ideas`**: Day 3, then every Monday
Turns the research into your own 10 reel launch plan. Real hooks from real reels with the view counts attached. Never one answer, always a menu.

**`ig-script`**: daily
Turns one idea into a film-ready script: hook, beats, on-screen text, caption, hashtags, filming spec. On build reels it also writes the step by step guide to build the thing you are demoing.

**`ig-review`**: every Friday
Paste your numbers. It tells you the truth: weak hook, suppressed account, wrong audience, or just too early.

## The three types of content

Everything you post is one of these:

**Their pain**: gets the reach. Your buyer's Monday morning on screen. A 288 follower account did 110,000 views with a guy at a desk watching calls go to voicemail.

**Your advice**: earns the trust. One thing they can use today, free, no pitch.

**Your build**: makes the money. Name the pain, show the fix working.

Roughly 50 / 30 / 20 while you are under 1,000 followers.

---

## The rules that matter most

1. **Steal the hook word for word.** Change the industry noun or the number. Nothing else. Rewrite it and you threw away the only tested part.
2. **Never post a build reel cold.** Post the pain first, within two days. Otherwise it is an ad and people scroll ads.
3. **One ICP only.** The algorithm cannot target an account it cannot classify.
4. **Never "comment X for Y" in the caption.** Most penalized pattern on Reels. Say it out loud in the video instead.
5. **Post from the phone. Nobody else logs in. No tools.**

## What normal looks like

Your first week will look bad. Almost every reel lands under 500 views because the account has no history yet. **Most people quit here.**

| Day | Followers |
|---|---|
| 7 | 10 to 50 |
| 14 | 50 to 200 |
| 21 | 150 to 600 |
| 30 | 300 to 1,500 |

The top of that range is what a focused cold start produced: 1,500 followers in 30 days, no existing audience, no ads.

**But followers are not the scoreboard.** Every Friday, open your new followers and check ten profiles. How many could buy from you? That is the number.
