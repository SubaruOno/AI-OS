# 01: Account Setup

Day 1. About an hour. Get this wrong and nothing else in the pack matters.

---

## Create it

**Account type: Personal.** Not Business. Not Creator, yet.

Start as Personal and stay there until Day 7. A brand new account that immediately becomes a business profile looks like what it is.

**Region and language:** set to the market you sell to. If you sell to US contractors, set United States and English.

**Email:** use a real email with history. A Gmail from years ago beats one you made ten minutes ago. Everything being brand new is itself a pattern.

**Phone:** a real carrier SIM. Never a VOIP number from Twilio or GoHighLevel, they get rejected.

**Username:** clean, memorable, related to what you do. No underscores, no numbers, no random characters if you can avoid it.

---

## The profile, day 1

Do not leave any of this for later. An empty profile gets no follows even when a reel does well.

### Profile photo
Your real face. Clear, well lit, close up.

Not a logo. Not an AI generated image. Not your dog. People buy from a person.

### Name field
**This is a search field, not your name.** Instagram indexes it.

- Bad: `Marcus Chen`
- Good: `Marcus | AI for HVAC Companies`

Put what you do and who you do it for. This is free ranking.

### Bio

Two lines. **Both are outcomes. Never mention AI.**

**Line 1 is your number one pain from the research, stated as fixed.** The thing they would say out loud at 9pm when they are annoyed.

**Line 2 is the result they get.** Not the technology. Not the method. The result.

```
Never lose another deal to the agent who called first.
Every lead gets a reply in 60 seconds, day or night.
```

| Selling | Bio |
|---|---|
| Call answering to HVAC | Stop losing jobs to a phone nobody answered.<br>Every call picked up. Every job booked. |
| Lead follow-up to gyms | Fill your classes without chasing anyone.<br>Every enquiry gets a reply and a trial booked. |
| Front desk automation for dental | Stop losing patients to voicemail.<br>Every call answered, every gap in the schedule filled. |
| Websites for contractors | Look like the company that charges more.<br>Live in 48 hours, built to turn visitors into calls. |

### Never mention AI

Your buyer does not want AI. They want their phone answered and their calendar full.

The second you say AI, three things happen. They think about cost. They think about it being complicated. They think about replacing staff. None of that helps you.

**Banned in the bio, the name field, and the first line of every caption:**

- AI, artificial intelligence, automation, bot, chatbot, agent
- The tool names. Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, Make, GoHighLevel
- "I help [niche] do [thing]"
- "AI automation for [niche]"
- "Helping [niche] scale with AI"

**Say the outcome instead:**

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| AI answers your calls | Every call gets answered |
| AI automation for your follow-up | Nobody waits more than a minute for a reply |
| AI chatbot on your website | Visitors get an answer at 11pm |
| Automated booking system | Your calendar fills itself |

**The test:** would your buyer nod at line one before they know what you sell? If they need to understand the technology first, rewrite it.

**The split.** Your name field is for search, so it carries the keywords. Your bio is for conversion, so it carries the outcome. Do not make them do the same job.

Grade three reading level. No hashtags. No emoji walls.

### The link
**Live before Day 4. Not later.**

This is the entire path from a reel to money. Someone watches, likes what they see, taps your profile, and either books or leaves. There is no third option.

Best to worst:
1. A booking link straight to your calendar
2. A short landing page with one button
3. A Linktree

Having any real link also signals a legitimate account rather than a throwaway.

### Story highlights
One to three, even basic. "About", "What I build", "Results". Empty covers are fine at first. It makes the profile look established instead of abandoned.

## Settings

**Turn ON**
- Suggested Accounts. Edit Profile, "allow others to find my account when they follow similar accounts". This is free distribution and most people leave it off.
- Contact syncing. Helps Instagram confirm you are a real person.

**Turn OFF**
- Activity Status. No reason to broadcast when you are online.

**Check**
- Settings, Account, Account Status. It tells you whether your content is eligible to be recommended. Check it now so you know your starting point, and check it any time your views collapse.

**Remove**
- Settings, Security, Apps and Websites. Remove every third party app. All of them.

## Day 7: switch to Creator

After your first two or three reels, switch to a Professional account and pick **Creator**.

**Creator, never Business.** Business accounts lose access to the trending music library. Trending audio is a real distribution lever on Reels, and giving it up for a "contact" button is a bad trade.

Switching is free, instant, reversible, and costs you no followers.

Category: Entrepreneur, Digital Creator, or Education. Any of them are fine.

---

## The rules

Break these and nothing else in this pack will save you.

### Post from the phone. Always.
No schedulers. No API posting. No automation tools. No "post from desktop" workarounds. Open the app, upload, post.

### Nobody else logs in. Ever.
Not a VA. Not your business partner. Not an agency. One account, one person, one device.

### One country, one network, for 90 days.
Logging in from different countries inside a short window is one of the fastest ways to get your reach throttled. It reads as a sold or stolen account.

If you travel, use your phone's own data or hotspot. Do not add a VPN. You do not need a proxy, and adding one creates the problem it claims to solve.

### One topic only.
Your ICP and their problems. Nothing else. No lifestyle posts, no gym content, no random reposts.

The algorithm cannot target an account it cannot classify. Mixed topics means no audience.

### Never "comment X for Y" in the caption
It is the most penalized caption pattern on Reels right now. On an established account it is tolerated. On a new one it caps your reach at your existing followers, which is nobody.

**Say your call to action out loud in the reel instead. Keep it out of the caption.**

### Nothing reused
Every video is filmed fresh. Instagram fingerprints video, and reposting something that already exists elsewhere gets it suppressed. Filters, crops and speed changes do not fool it.

### Export through the Instagram Edits app
Edit wherever you want, but do the final export through Edits. Instagram confirmed a distribution boost for content exported through it. It is free and it takes one extra tap.
