# 02: Warmup

Days 1 to 3. About 30 minutes a day. No posting.

This is the part everyone skips and it is the part that decides who sees your reels.

---

## What warmup actually is

Instagram has no idea who you are on day one. It does not know what your account is about or who to show it to. It works that out by watching what you do.

**Warmup is you telling the algorithm who your buyer is, before you ever post.**

Skip it and your first reel goes out to a random slice of nobody. Do it properly and your first reel goes out to people who own the kind of business you sell to.

Three days. That is the whole cost.

---

## Get your target list first

Run the research before you start warming up. You need the account list.

```
/ig-icp-research
```

It gives you 30 to 50 handles split into two pools. That list is what you engage with. Without it you are guessing at who your buyer follows, and guessing trains the algorithm on the wrong people.

---

## The two pools

### ICP pool: 70% of your engagement

Accounts your buyer actually follows. Industry news pages, trade humour accounts, equipment and software brands, the coaches who advise them.

**This is the pool that matters.** Every like, save and comment here tells Instagram "show my stuff to people like this."

### Vendor pool: 30% of your engagement

The consultants, agencies and software companies already selling into your niche. This tells Instagram what kind of account you are.

---

## The mindset

**"I am my buyer. What would I engage with?"**

If you sell to HVAC owners, scroll like an HVAC owner. Watch what they would watch to the end. Save what they would save. Laugh at what they would laugh at.

You are not browsing. You are training a machine, and every action is a training example.

---

## Days 1 to 3: build the pattern

Two or three sessions a day, 10 to 15 minutes each. Real time, real attention. Do not speed run it.

**Do**
- Follow 5 to 10 accounts a day from your list. Real, active ones only.
- Like 3 to 5 posts per session
- **Watch reels all the way to the end.** Watch time is the strongest signal you can send.
- Save posts your buyer would save
- Tap through Stories naturally

**Do not**
- Post anything. Nothing for the first 72 hours.
- Comment. Zero comments in the first 48 hours. Commenting immediately on a fresh account is a flag.
- Send DMs
- Follow more than 10 accounts a day
- Like more than 15 posts a day
- Use any tool, bot or automation for any of this

### Why the caps

Instagram's systems spot automated patterns within hours. Moving too fast can drop your reach 40 to 90% overnight, and shadowbans from early aggressive behaviour last one to three weeks.

You are not saving time by rushing. You are burning the account.

---

## Days 4 to 7: comments open

You start posting on Day 4. Engagement steps up with it.

- Comment on 5 to 10 posts a day
- **8 words minimum, real sentences.** Never "🔥" or "great post". Say something a person in that industry would say.
- Likes up to 15 to 25 a day
- Keep total daily actions under 150

Comments in your ICP pool are the highest value thing you can do. Your buyer reads comments on the accounts they follow. That is how they find you before they ever see a reel.

---

## After you start posting

Every day, right after your reel goes up:

**15 minutes of engagement.** Same two pools, same ratio.

**Reply to every comment on your own reel inside the first hour.** Reply speed is a ranking signal, and the first hour decides how far the reel travels.

---

## How to know it worked

You are ready to post when:

- Your Explore feed is full of your buyer's world, not random content
- Reels from your niche show up without you searching
- You are getting a few follows back from real accounts in the industry

**Explore is the readout.** If after three days it is still showing you general content, your engagement was too shallow. Give it another day and go deeper: longer watch time, more saves, more real comments.

---

## If you get an action block

Instagram tells you that you are doing something too often.

1. Cut all activity by half immediately
2. Wait 24 to 48 hours
3. Resume slowly

**Do not try to push through.** Blocks escalate, and the next one lasts longer.

---

## Warmup never really ends

The 3 day version is intense because you are starting from zero. After that it becomes 15 minutes a day, forever.

The day you stop engaging is the day the algorithm starts forgetting who you are for.

---

## Checklist

**Day 1**
- [ ] Research run, target list in hand
- [ ] 2 to 3 sessions, 10 to 15 min
- [ ] 5 to 10 follows, 3 to 5 likes per session
- [ ] Zero comments, zero posts

**Day 2**
- [ ] Same again, different accounts
- [ ] Watching reels to the end

**Day 3**
- [ ] Same again
- [ ] Explore feed now looks like your buyer's feed
- [ ] Launch plan generated, first reel filmed

**Day 4**
- [ ] Comments open, 8 words minimum
- [ ] First reel posted
- [ ] 15 minutes engagement after posting
- [ ] Reply to every comment inside the hour
