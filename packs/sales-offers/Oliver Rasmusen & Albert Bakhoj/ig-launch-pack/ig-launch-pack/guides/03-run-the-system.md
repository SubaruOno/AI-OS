# 03: Run The System

The 30 days. What you do, in order.

---

## Time cost, honestly

**45 to 90 minutes a day.**

Filming a reel is 15 to 30 minutes. A build reel is longer because you are building something real. Engagement is 15 minutes. Editing is 15 to 20.

Anyone who tells you this takes 10 minutes a day is selling you something. It is one hour a day for 30 days. That is the deal.

---

## The 30 days

### Day 1: Research and setup

```
/ig-icp-research
```

Answer two questions: what you sell, who you sell it to.

**Wait for the verdict before you do anything else.**

- **GREEN**: continue
- **AMBER**: continue, but expect a slower start and lean on advice content
- **RED**: **stop.** Your buyer is not reachable on Instagram, or the content in your niche is aimed at your buyer's customers instead of your buyer. Do not spend 30 days finding this out the hard way. Pick a different ICP, or take the pain list you just generated and put it into cold email instead. The research is still worth having.

Then set the account up (guide 01) and start warmup (guide 02).

### Days 1 to 3: Warmup only

No posting. Follow, like, watch reels to the end. Two or three short sessions a day.

By Day 3 your Explore feed should look like your buyer's feed.

### Day 3: Get your launch plan

```
/ig-ideas
```

Launch mode. Out comes your own 10 reel plan for Days 4 to 13. Hooks word for word from real reels, with view counts attached. Captions, hashtags, why each slot exists.

Then:

```
/ig-script
```

Script Day 4. Film it tonight.

### Day 4: First reel

Post at your chosen time. Around 6pm Europe or 11am to 1pm US Eastern covers both markets.

Then 15 minutes of engagement, and reply to every comment inside the first hour.

**Your first reel will probably do under 500 views. That is normal.** The account has no history to rank on. Members quit here. Do not be one of them.

### Days 4 to 13: One reel a day

Follow the launch plan. Every slot has a job.

| Day | Type |
|---|---|
| 4 | Their pain |
| 5 | Your advice |
| 6 | Their pain |
| 7 | Your build · **switch to Creator account today** |
| 8 | Their pain |
| 9 | Your advice |
| 10 | Your build · flagship |
| 11 | Their pain |
| 12 | Your advice |
| 13 | Your build |

Days 8, 9 and 10 are the sequence that sells. Pain, then the manual fix, then your automation. You are not pitching. You are removing something they just agreed hurts.

### Every Friday

```
/ig-review
```

Paste your numbers. It tells you whether a reel was weak, the account is suppressed, or the wrong people are following.

**Before you run it, open your new followers and look at ten profiles. How many could actually buy from you?** That number matters more than views.

### Days 14 to 30: The weekly loop

**Monday**: `/ig-ideas` for the week's menu. Pick seven.
**Daily**: `/ig-script`, film, post, engage 15 minutes.
**Friday**: `/ig-review`.

---

## Film daily or batch. Your call.

**Daily.** Build it, film it, post it, same day. Tightest learning loop, and your build reels are genuinely fresh because you just did the thing.

**Batch.** Script five to seven on Sunday, film them in one sitting, post one a day. Change your shirt between takes so it does not look like one session.

Most people who fail at daily posting fail at daily filming, not daily posting. If you are unsure, batch.

---

## When a reel takes off

Break the plan.

- Part two within 48 hours while the topic is still being pushed
- Reply to every comment for two days, not one hour
- Do not rush out a build reel to cash in. Post the same pain from another angle first and milk the reach.

Momentum beats the calendar. The calendar is for when there is no momentum.

## When you miss a day

Post the next day. Do not post two to catch up.

One missed day is nothing. Three days of silence signals an abandoned account and your reach drops.

## Repurpose to TikTok and Shorts

Same vertical video, three platforms, about 10 extra minutes.

**Do**
- Export the clean file before adding Instagram text, or use a watermark remover. Platforms suppress each other's watermarks.
- Rewrite the caption per platform. TikTok is looser, Shorts wants the keyword in the title.
- Post at the same time each day.

**Do not**
- Cross-post the Instagram file with the logo still on it
- Chase all three at once in week one. Instagram first. Add the others in week 3 when the workflow is boring.

TikTok in particular still gives new accounts real reach. It is close to free.

## What to expect

| Day | Followers | Views per reel |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 10 to 50 | 100 to 400 |
| 14 | 50 to 200 | 300 to 1,000 |
| 21 | 150 to 600 | 500 to 2,000 |
| 30 | 300 to 1,500 | Wide. One breakout changes everything. |

The top of that range is what a focused cold start produced: **1,500 followers in 30 days, no audience, no ads.**

If you land at 400 followers and 40 of them own businesses you can sell to, you are ahead. **You are building a room of buyers, not an audience.**

---

## The five ways people fail

1. **Skipping warmup.** The algorithm never learns who you are for, and every reel goes to the wrong people.
2. **Quitting on day 8.** The first week always looks bad. It is not information yet.
3. **Rewriting the hooks.** You threw away the only tested part.
4. **Drifting into AI content.** "Look at this AI tool" reaches other agency people, not buyers. Stay on your buyer's problems.
5. **Posting build reels cold.** Without the pain first, it is an ad, and people scroll ads.

## What you actually walk away with

Thirty days of this and you own:

- An account in front of real buyers
- Ten or more working assets you built and can sell
- A pain list in your buyer's own words that feeds your cold email, your cold calls, your website and your sales calls
- Proof on camera that you can do the thing

The reels are the visible part. The rest is the business.
