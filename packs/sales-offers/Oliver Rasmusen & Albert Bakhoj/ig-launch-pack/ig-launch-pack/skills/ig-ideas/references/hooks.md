# Hooks

The first three seconds decide everything. A reel with a 60%+ hold rate at three seconds outperforms a weak hook by 5 to 10 times on total reach. Same body, same offer, same account.

This is the highest leverage part of the whole system, and it is also the part people ruin.

---

## The one rule: frame is fixed, slot is yours

Every proven hook has two parts.

**The frame** is the sentence shape that did the work. Untouchable.
**The slot** is the bit that names an industry, a number, a job title or a pain. That is where your ICP goes.

```
Frame:  "5 reasons your [SLOT] isn't [SLOT]"
Proven: "5 reasons your RDH isn't 3k a day"
Yours:  "5 reasons your techs aren't 3k a day"
```

```
Frame:  "You don't have a [SLOT] problem. You have a [SLOT] problem."
Proven: "You don't have a lead problem. You have a leaving the house problem."
Yours:  "You don't have a lead problem. You have a reply speed problem."
```

```
Frame:  "When someone asks my biggest fear and it's [SLOT] but I can't say
         that so I just say snakes"
Proven: slot = "buyers who want to work with [lender]"
Yours:  slot = "a lead who filled out the form six hours ago"
```

Filling the slot is correct, even when the slot is long. **Touching the frame is not.**

**Banned:** "Here are five reasons your technicians might not be hitting their revenue targets."

That kept the idea and threw away the frame. It will do 300 views.

Every hook handed to you prints its frame and its slot. If a hook arrives without them marked, it was not taken from a real reel. Do not film it.

**If you rewrite the frame, you threw away the only tested thing you had.**

---

## Why paraphrasing kills it

Proven hooks are usually ugly. They have weird rhythm. They start mid-thought. They break grammar.

"POV: How it feels calling leads in the morning."
"Nobody tells you what owning a 24-hour gym actually involves. So I will:"
"Brazing rods are HOW much??"

Every instinct you have will want to clean those up. Cleaning them up is what makes them stop working. The roughness is the reason they feel like a real person instead of an ad.

Leave them ugly.

Each one is followed by a real reel. Watch it before you use the pattern.

### POV
> "POV: How it feels calling leads in the morning"
> "POV: When it's 4:29 on a Friday and another no A/C call comes in"

### Nobody tells you

### The number list

The number sets a finish line so people stay to the end. [233.7K views, 21.8x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaJmIjJNDft/)

### Don't do X, do Y

Immediately useful. This is the highest-trust hook pattern there is. [65.8K views, 36.8x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaiWGF0FOGJ/)

### The wrong question

Argues with something your buyer already believes. Stops the scroll through disagreement. [33.5K views, 23.8x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da1fHyljckd/)

### The gap
> "The difference between the dentist who makes $1 million a year and the ones who make $250k"

Two numbers, one gap. Everyone wants to be on the right side of it. [96.3K views, 20.5x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaTG_lluNKs/)

### Wrong place
> "Most gym owners are advertising in the wrong place. They spend thousands hoping someone books."

Names a specific waste of money. Sets up your offer without pitching. [1.3K followers, 46.7K views, 76.6x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaVhkc1BzqE/)

### The qualifier

Filters in the first two seconds. Views drop, buyer density goes way up. Use on build reels. [46.5K views, 17.6x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaWVFdkg2yi/)

## Where your hooks come from

Never from your head. Never from this file alone.

They come from `ig-ideas`, which pulls live outliers in your niche and captures the exact wording. This file exists so you can recognize the patterns, not so you can copy from it.

A hook with no view count attached is a guess. Do not film a guess.

---

## Steal the hook. Never the concept.

Same hook, your buyer, your pain, your build = a different reel.

Same hook, same concept, same tool, same script = a duplicate. Instagram's duplicate detection will find it and bury both of you.

If everyone in the community makes the reel the tool suggested first, nobody gets reach. Pick from the menu. Choose the one that fits your offer, not the one with the biggest number.

---

## On-screen text

Your hook goes in three places at once, and it must match in all three:

1. **Spoken** in the first three seconds
2. **On screen** as text, large, top third, readable at a glance
3. **First line of the caption**

Most people watch on mute. If the hook is not on screen, it does not exist.

---

## The three-second test

Before you film, ask: does the first line name something my buyer felt this week?

If the answer is "it explains what I do," you do not have a hook. You have an intro. Cut it and start on the pain.
