# 6. WHEN SEARCH RUNS DRY

**The suggested chain.** Open a good account, tap the down arrow next to Follow, Instagram shows similar accounts. Open one, repeat. Ten minutes of this beats any search.

**Comment mining.** Open a big reel in the niche, read comments, tap the profiles of people arguing. Those are your buyers.

**Tagged posts.** On a vendor account, check tagged photos. Their clients are your prospects.

**Audio pages.** Tap the audio on a reel that worked. You get every reel using that sound. Fastest way to find a format spreading right now.

# 8. JUDGE IT

| vs that account's normal | Verdict |
|---|---|
| under 3x | skip |
| **5x to 200x** | **copy this** |
| over 500x | lottery ticket, do not copy |

Small account with a big multiplier beats a big account with a small one.

# 9. CAPTURE IT

1. Play the first 3 seconds
2. Screenshot
3. Write the on-screen text **word for word**
4. Write the spoken line **word for word**
5. Share button, copy link

**Never paraphrase.** Proven hooks are ugly. Leave them ugly.
