# The Three Content Types

Every reel you make is one of three types. Each does a different job. Mixing them wrong is why most accounts stall.

Every example below is a real reel with real numbers, pulled July 2026. Go watch them.

---

## TYPE 1: THEIR PAIN

**Job:** reach. This is what gets you in front of your buyer.

**Why it works:** your buyer sees their own Monday morning on screen. They stop, they laugh, they send it to their business partner. Shares are the strongest signal on Reels.

**You do not sell anything in these. At all.**

### Proof

| Reel | Followers | Views | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| [POV: How it feels calling leads in the morning](https://www.tiktok.com/@dag_mawee/video/7657201805327207710) | 288 | 110K | 21.7x |
| [4:29 on a Friday and another no A/C call comes in](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Danp_l-C3jk/) | 390 | 192.2K | 108x |
| [Nobody tells you what owning a 24-hour gym actually involves](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbDh3NBPc2l/) | 888 | 632.6K | 27.9x |
| [If trainers cancelled classes the same way members did](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DabEv7ohNHX/) | 4.5K | 875.5K | 118.5x |
| [POV: front desk, why don't y'all know your insurance](https://www.tiktok.com/@styleyourbeautytv/video/7660218446151437581) | 11K | 396K | 187.7x |

Look at the follower counts. 288. 390. 888. This type does not need an audience.

### Sub-structures

**POV skit.** "POV: [the situation]." You act out the moment. 7 to 20 seconds. Static camera, one take.
*Reference: dag_mawee. A man at a desk. Phone goes to voicemail. That is the whole reel.*

**Deadpan reaction.** Text on screen states the pain. You just look at the camera. No dialogue. 5 to 10 seconds.
*Lowest effort reel that exists. Film ten in ten minutes.*

**Phone call reenactment.** You act both sides of a call your buyer has every week. 15 to 30 seconds.

**The rant.** Sit in your car. Talk about the thing that drives your buyer insane. 30 to 60 seconds.
*Reference: styleyourbeautytv did 396K from a parked car.*

**The honest list.** "Nobody tells you what [running this business] actually involves. So I will:" then 5 to 7 real items, text on screen over b-roll.
*Reference: moreready24, 888 followers, 632K views.*

### Filming requirements

Nearly zero. A desk, a phone, a car, or a wall. If a pain comedy idea needs a job site, a second actor you do not have, or props, throw it out and pick another.

### The trap

**Do not fake the pain.** If you have never run this business, you will get the details wrong and your buyer will feel it in half a second. Every pain you use must come from your research file, in their words, with a view count proving it lands.

## TYPE 2: YOUR ADVICE

**Job:** trust. This is what turns a viewer into a follower.

**Why it works:** you give them something they can use today, for free, with no pitch. They conclude you know their business. That is the whole point.

### Proof

| Reel | Followers | Views | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Don't ask new patients IF they want to book. Offer choices](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaiWGF0FOGJ/) | 76K | 65.8K | 36.8x |
| [5 reasons your RDH isn't 3k a day](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaJmIjJNDft/) | 28.7K | 233.7K | 21.8x |
| [Everyone asks which HVAC brand is best. Wrong question](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da1fHyljckd/) | 8.8K | 33.5K | 23.8x |
| [The difference between dentists making $1M and $250k](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaTG_lluNKs/) | 28.7K | 96.3K | 20.5x |
| [Most gym owners are advertising in the wrong place](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaVhkc1BzqE/) | 1.3K | 46.7K | 76.6x |
| [30 sessions a week should be the bare minimum](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dali7x4yTF9/) | 5.1K | 45.3K | 14x |

### Sub-structures

**The script handout.** Give them exact words to say. "Don't ask if they want to book. Say: Thursday or Monday?" 20 to 40 seconds.
*Highest value per second. Your buyer can use it in the next hour.*

**Numbered reasons.** "5 reasons your [thing] isn't [outcome]." 30 to 60 seconds. Works because it promises a finish line.

**The contrarian take.** "Everyone thinks X. The real answer is Y." Stops the scroll because it argues with something they believe.

**The comparison.** "The difference between the ones who make $1M and the ones who make $250k."

**Wrong place, right place.** "You are spending money on X. It should go to Y." Sets up your offer without pitching it.

### Filming requirements

Talking head. Car, desk, or standing. Static camera. Your face and your voice.

### The trap

**The advice has to be real.** Your buyer runs this business every day. Generic advice reads as an outsider guessing, and it is worse than posting nothing. Source it from the research file, from actual operators, or from what the winning accounts in your niche already say. Never invent it.

## TYPE 3: YOUR BUILD

**Job:** money. This is the reel that fills your calendar.

**Why it works:** you name a pain they already felt in your last reel, then show the fix working on screen. No abstraction. They watch a problem disappear.

### Proof

| Reel | Followers | Views | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| ["This is Nina", missed call, lost job, AI answers it](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaN3JPSgzj7/) | 6.2K | 29.8K | 39.1x |
| [The Claude AI agent that's worth $30,000](https://www.tiktok.com/@structurewebworks/video/7658340467003690258) | 11.6K | 29.6K | 17.5x |
| [If you run a home service business doing over $50k a month](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaWVFdkg2yi/) | 4.9K | 46.5K | 17.6x |

Lower view counts than the other two types. That is correct and expected. These reels are not for reach. They are for the four people watching who own the business and just realized this exists.

### Sub-structures

**Problem to solution narrative.** Open on a person losing money. Show the loss. Reveal the fix. 30 to 45 seconds.
*Reference: "This is Nina." She misses a call. She loses the job. The AI answers it. That structure is worth memorizing.*

**Screen-recorded build.** You build the thing live on screen with voiceover. 40 to 60 seconds. Fast cuts, no dead air.

**The number reveal.** "This costs them $4,000 a month in missed jobs. This fixes it for $500." Borrowed proof only, see below.

**Qualifier open.** "If you run a home service business doing over fifty thousand a month..." Filters the audience in the first two seconds so only buyers keep watching.

### Filming requirements

Screen recording plus your voice. Your face optional. Voice is not optional, it is what makes someone trust you enough to book.

### How much to reveal

**Mirror the source reel.** If the proven reel gave the full recipe, give the full recipe. If it showed the outcome and only part of the method, do the same. It worked on this audience for them. Do not invent a policy the evidence does not support.

Two constants either way: always show the outcome completely, and never put credentials, API keys or client data on screen.

### The rule

**Every build reel must be preceded within two days by the pain it solves.** Post a build reel cold and it reads as an ad. Post it after they felt the problem and it reads as a solution.

## THE VOICE RULE: acting is fine, claiming is not

**The hook never changes. The virality lives in the exact words.** Anything in this section that would rewrite a frame is wrong.

You do not run the business you sell to. That matters in exactly one place, and it is much narrower than it first looks.

### Playing a character is not lying

Most pain hooks are dialogue or a scene. You are acting. Nobody thinks a comedian claiming to be a client is a client.

> "We already have a Realtor... can you just look over this contract?"

That line is spoken **by the client**, not the agent. Play the client. **Change nothing.** Rewriting this into "every agent has had this call" throws away the frame that made it work and it will die.

Same for any reenactment, any two-hander, any POV where you are voicing someone else.

### The only thing that breaks you

**Claiming experience or credentials as yourself, in your own voice.**

> "After closing 75+ homes by 26, I realized everyone who fails in real estate does 1 of these 3 things"

That is a resume claim. You have not closed 75 homes. Do not film it, do not adapt it, drop the whole reel. That is the reel you get caught on, and you get caught on the sales call, not in the comments.

### The one word fix

Some hooks carry a first person possessive that claims an identity. That word is a slot. Swap it and keep everything else.

| Original | Yours | Change |
|---|---|---|
| "POV: Trying to get **my** 89 year old clients to sign that DocuSign" | "POV: Trying to get **your** 89 year old client to sign that DocuSign" | one word |
| "**We** already have a Realtor, can you just look over this contract?" | unchanged, you are playing the client | none |
| "When someone asks **my** biggest fear..." | unchanged if you are acting the scene | none |

One word. Never a rewritten sentence.

### The vendor accounts that win

Worth knowing which register they use, because it is the safest ground when you are speaking as yourself:

- @nextiva: "This is Nina." Third person story.
- @akoolastudios: "If you run a home service business doing over fifty thousand a month."
- @kennydonnelldfw: "5 unprofessional realtor habits that are costing you clients."
- @easydigitalmarketing500: "Most gym owners are advertising in the wrong place."

All second or third person. All perfectly usable by a vendor with zero changes.

### The check

**Am I acting a character, or am I claiming a life I have not lived?**

Acting: film it exactly as written.
Claiming: one word swap if a pronoun does it, otherwise drop the reel.

Never touch the frame to solve this. There is always another reel.

## NEVER LEAD WITH AI

Your buyer does not want AI. They want the phone answered and the calendar full.

Say AI in the first three seconds and they think three things: expensive, complicated, and someone is getting fired. None of that helps you.

**Banned in the bio, the name field, the hook, and the first line of every caption:**

`AI` · `artificial intelligence` · `automation` · `bot` · `chatbot` · `agent` · `Claude` · `ChatGPT` · `n8n` · `Make` · `GoHighLevel` · any other tool name

**Allowed, and encouraged:** on screen inside a build reel, and in the back half of a reel once they have felt the problem. Showing is fine. Leading is not.

### This is what the winning vendor accounts do

| Account | Opens with | Sells |
|---|---|---|
| [@nextiva](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaN3JPSgzj7/) | "This is Nina." A woman misses a call. | an AI receptionist |
| [@akoolastudios](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaWVFdkg2yi/) | "If you run a home service business doing over fifty thousand a month" | lead generation |
| [@easydigitalmarketing500](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaVhkc1BzqE/) | "Most gym owners are advertising in the wrong place" | ad management |

Every one leads with the buyer's problem. The mechanism is the reveal, never the hook.

### Swap table

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| AI answers your calls | Every call gets answered |
| AI automation for follow-up | Nobody waits more than a minute for a reply |
| AI chatbot on your site | Visitors get an answer at 11pm |
| Automated booking | Your calendar fills itself |
| I built an AI agent | I built the thing that books your jobs |

### The one exception

A proven hook that names a tool, where the source's audience is people who want to use the tool themselves.

[@coffeeandcontracts](https://www.tiktok.com/@coffeeandcontracts/video/7656929815316024606) did 51.8x with "how to connect Claude to the MLS". That works because their audience is agents who want to do it themselves.

**That is not your buyer.** You sell done-for-you. Keep the frame, drop the tool name from the slot:

`how to connect Claude to the MLS` becomes `how to get every lead answered in 60 seconds`

If a hook only works because it names the tool, it is the wrong hook for this account. There is always another reel.

## The mix

While under 1,000 followers:

- **50% their pain**: you need reach before anything else matters
- **30% your advice**: this is what converts a viewer into a follower
- **20% your build**: this is what converts a follower into a call

As your audience gets denser with real buyers, shift toward more build reels.

## The three rules that break accounts when ignored

1. **Never the same type two days running.** The algorithm needs variety to keep classifying you.
2. **Never two builds back to back.** Your account becomes an ad and people unfollow.
3. **One ICP only.** Do not jump between dentists and HVAC. The algorithm cannot target an account it cannot classify.
