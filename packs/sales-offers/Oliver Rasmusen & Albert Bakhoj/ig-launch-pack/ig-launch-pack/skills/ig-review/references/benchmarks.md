# Benchmarks

What normal actually looks like. Read this before you decide the system is broken.

---

## The honest framing

You are building a small audience of buyers, not a big audience of watchers. Your numbers will look worse than an AI tools account and be worth more.

**A 3,000 view reel that brings four HVAC owners beats a 40,000 view reel that brings four hundred teenagers.** Judge yourself on that, always.

---

## New account view bands

A brand new account with no audience. Views per reel, first 30 days.

| Percentile | Views per reel | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom | Under 200 | Something is wrong. Check the suppression list below. |
| Low | 200 to 500 | Normal for days 4 to 8. Concerning after day 14. |
| Normal | 500 to 2,000 | Where most reels land. This is fine. |
| Good | 2,000 to 10,000 | The format is working. Make more like it. |
| Breakout | 10,000+ | Drop the calendar. Milk it. Part two within 48 hours. |

**Days 4 to 7 will look bad.** Almost every reel lands under 500. The account is brand new and has no history to rank on. This is not failure, it is the cost of entry. Members who quit, quit here.

## Follower bands

| Day | Realistic range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Day 7 | 10 to 50 | Almost entirely from your own engagement |
| Day 14 | 50 to 200 | First reel with real reach usually lands here |
| Day 21 | 150 to 600 | Compounding starts |
| Day 30 | 300 to 1,500 | Enormous spread. One breakout reel is the whole difference. |

The reference point: a cold start account with no audience push and no ads reached **1,500 followers in 30 days**. That is the top of this range, not the middle. If you land at 400 with 40 of them being real buyers, you are ahead of where you think.

## Multiplier bands

Multiplier means views divided by your own median. More useful than raw views because it is relative to you.

- **Under 1x**: below your own average. Weak hook.
- **1x to 3x**: normal spread.
- **3x to 10x**: the format worked. Repeat the structure.
- **10x+**: outlier. This is the one to build a part two on.

Every reel that beat 14x in our research came from an account under 30,000 followers. Multipliers are not a big-account thing.

## Diagnosis: is it the reel or the account?

These have opposite fixes. Getting this wrong wastes weeks.

### The reel underperformed
- Some reels do fine, some do badly
- Your best reel is 5x or more above your worst
- Views vary a lot week to week

**Fix: make more reels.** Your hooks are inconsistent. Post through it, use only hooks with proven view counts attached, and stop rewriting them.

### The account is suppressed
- Every reel lands in the same narrow, low band regardless of quality
- A reel you know is strong still does 200
- No reel has broken 500 in ten or more posts
- Numbers dropped off a cliff and never recovered

**Fix: stop posting and check the account.**

1. Settings, Account, Account Status. Are you eligible to be recommended?
2. Have you used any scheduler, bot, or third party tool? Remove all app access.
3. Has anyone else logged in? Has your IP or country changed?
4. Are your captions using "comment X for Y"? That is the most penalized caption pattern on Reels. Stop.
5. Have you posted anything reused from another account? Duplicate detection catches it.

Cut activity in half for 48 hours, fix the cause, then resume. Do not push through a block. They escalate.

## The metric that actually matters

Every Friday, ignore views first and answer this:

**Of the people who followed me this week, how many could actually buy what I sell?**

Check it by hand. Open your new followers and look at ten profiles.

- **Mostly your ICP**: the system is working. Keep going even if views are low.
- **Mostly other agency people and AI accounts**: your content is aimed at the wrong pool. You are making "look at this cool AI" reels instead of "here is your Monday morning" reels. Go back to pain content.
- **Mostly nobody, random accounts**: your content has no clear audience. Tighten to one ICP and one topic.

This one check is worth more than every other number on this page.

## Secondary signals

**Saves and shares beat likes.** A reel with 50 saves and 20 shares outperforms one with 200 likes. Shares are the strongest ranking signal on Reels.

**Watch time and skip rate.** If people leave in the first 3 seconds, the hook failed. That is the fastest way to kill distribution.

**Profile visits.** More important than views. It means someone wanted to know who you are.

**DMs from the ICP.** The real scoreboard. One DM from a business owner beats 10,000 views.

---

## When to actually worry

- **Day 14, nothing has broken 500 views**: check for suppression, not content
- **Day 21, followers are not your ICP**: your content is aimed wrong, fix the pool
- **Day 30, zero profile visits from real businesses**: the ICP may not be reachable on Instagram. Reconsider the niche or move the effort to cold email.

Everything else is too early to call.
