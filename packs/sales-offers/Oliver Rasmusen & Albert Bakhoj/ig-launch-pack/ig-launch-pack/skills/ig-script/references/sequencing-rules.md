# Sequencing Rules

What to post on which day, and why. These rules exist because the algorithm needs to classify your account fast, and because your buyer needs to feel a problem before they care about a solution.

---

## The launch calendar: Days 4 to 13

Ten reels. One a day. Every slot has a job.

| Day | Type | What it is | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Their pain | Your ICP's most universal daily frustration | Widest sampling. Zero barrier. Tells the algorithm who this account is for. |
| 5 | Your advice | One fix they can use today | Different signal from Day 4. Earns the first real follows. |
| 6 | Their pain | A second pain, different scenario | Reinforces the classification while it is still forming |
| 7 | Your build | The smallest thing you build | First reveal of what you sell. Switch to Creator account today. |
| 8 | Their pain | The exact pain your offer removes | Sets up Day 9 and Day 10 |
| 9 | Your advice | The manual fix for that same pain | Proves you understand the problem before you sell the automation |
| 10 | Your build | The flagship, full walkthrough | Highest intent reel of the launch. Your booking link earns its keep today. |
| 11 | Their pain | Money pain: lost jobs, price pressure, no-shows | Reach reset after two heavy reels |
| 12 | Your advice | Pricing and market rates, borrowed proof | Depth and trust without claiming results you do not have |
| 13 | Your build | Second build, different problem | Shows range. Review your numbers, plan week 3. |

Four pain, three advice, three build.

Notice Days 8, 9 and 10. Pain, then the manual fix, then the automation. That is the sequence that sells. You are not pitching, you are removing something they just admitted hurts.

---

## Rules that carry past Day 13

**1. Never the same type two days running.**
The algorithm classifies you by variety inside a consistent topic. Same topic every day, different format every day.

**2. Never two build reels back to back.**
Two in a row and your account reads as an ad. People unfollow ads.

**3. Every build reel needs its pain within two days before it.**
Cold build reel: an ad. Build reel after the pain: a solution. Same footage, completely different result.

**4. Rotate the pain.**
Phone and missed calls. Staffing and techs quitting. No-shows and cancellations. Pricing and the lowest bidder. Admin and paperwork. Reviews and reputation.

Never the same pain twice in a week. Your buyer has more than one problem and the algorithm needs the range to find more of them.

**5. One ICP only.**
Do not post for dentists on Monday and HVAC on Tuesday. The algorithm cannot target an account it cannot classify, and you will get nobody.

If you genuinely serve two niches, pick one for the first 90 days. Add the second later, from a second account.

**6. Roughly 50 / 30 / 20 under 1,000 followers.**
Half pain, a third advice, a fifth build. Shift toward more build reels once your followers are actually buyers.

---

## After Day 13

**Monday.** Run `ig-ideas`. Get the week's menu. Pick seven.
**Tuesday to Sunday.** One reel a day. Film daily or batch on Sunday.
**Friday.** Run `ig-review`. Paste your numbers. Adjust.

Same rules apply. The calendar just repeats with fresh outliers.

---

## When something takes off

If a reel goes far past your normal numbers, break the calendar.

- Make a part two within 48 hours while the topic is still being pushed
- Reply to every comment for the first hour, then keep replying for two days
- Do not immediately post a build reel to cash in. Post the pain again from a different angle. Milk the reach first.

Momentum beats the grid. The grid is what you follow when there is no momentum.

---

## When you miss a day

Post the next day. Do not post two to catch up.

Gaps of three or more days signal an abandoned account and reach drops. One missed day is nothing. Three is a problem.

---

## Posting time

One reel a day at the overlap window for your market. Around 6pm in Europe, around 11am to 1pm US Eastern, covers both.

Pick one time and keep it. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.
