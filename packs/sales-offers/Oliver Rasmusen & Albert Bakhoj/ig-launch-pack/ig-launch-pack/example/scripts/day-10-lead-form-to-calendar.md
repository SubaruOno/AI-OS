## How much to show

**This is the part people get wrong.**

The source teaches agents to do it themselves. You are not selling a tutorial, you are selling the done version. So:

- **Show the outcome in full.** Lead in, reply out, showing booked. All of it, on screen.
- **Show enough of the build to prove it is real.** The connection, the questions it asks, the live reply.
- **Do not hand over the full recipe.** No API keys on screen, no complete prompt, no step by step anyone could rebuild from a pause.

You want them thinking "that is exactly my problem and I am not building that." Not "I will try that Saturday."

# THE BUILD GUIDE

**What you are building:** an AI that watches one lead source, texts every new lead within 60 seconds, asks one qualifying question, and books a showing on your calendar.

**Time:** 60 to 90 minutes the first time.
**You need:** a Twilio or similar SMS number, a calendar, and an automation tool (n8n, Make, or GoHighLevel).

## Step 1: Pick one lead source

One only. A Facebook lead form or a website form. Do not start with Zillow, the integration is slower to get approved and you will stall on day one.

**Record this:** the form itself, and a test lead landing.

## Step 2: Get a phone number

Buy an SMS-capable number in your client's area code. Local numbers get replies. Toll free numbers do not.

**Record this:** nothing. Skip it in the reel, it is boring.

## Step 3: Build the trigger

New form submission fires the workflow. Add a 30 second delay so it feels human rather than instant.

**Record this:** the workflow canvas. Node names on screen read well on camera.

## Step 4: Write the message

One question. Not five. The most common mistake is asking for everything at once and getting nothing back.

```
Hi [name], it's [agent] with [brokerage]. Saw you were looking at
[property]. Are you hoping to see it this week or still early in
your search?
```

Short. Human. One question. An easy yes.

**Record this:** the message arriving on a real phone. This is the money shot of the whole reel.

## Step 5: Handle the reply

Two branches only:
- **"This week"** → send the calendar link
- **Anything else** → tag as nurture, follow up in 3 days

Do not build a conversation tree on day one. Two branches converts fine and ships today.

**Record this:** the reply coming in and the branch firing.

## Step 6: Book it

Calendar link in the reply. Booking confirmed goes to the agent by text.

**Record this:** the calendar filling in. Close the reel on it.

## Step 7: Test it properly

Submit a real test lead from your own phone. Watch the whole chain end to end. Time it.

**If it takes longer than 60 seconds, cut the delay, not the quality.**

Run it three times before you film. You are filming a proof, and a proof that breaks on camera is worse than no reel.

## What this is worth

Lead follow-up and CRM tools in real estate run roughly $300 to $1,500 a month. You are building the done-for-you version of the same job.

Sensible first pricing: $750 setup, $300 a month.

Use market rates, never invented results. "Tools like this run $300 to $1,500 a month" is true and checkable. "I made an agent $40,000" is not, and it will fall apart on the first call.

## What you own after this

A working lead responder you can demo live on any sales call, rebuild for the next client in about 20 minutes, and sell for the rest of the month.

**The reel is the by-product. This is the asset.**
