## ROI Formulas

- Hidden cost of a process = people involved x hours per week x loaded hourly rate x 52
- Loaded hourly rate = salary / 1,750 x 1.3 (example loading)
- Payback period = build cost / monthly savings
- Always quote the client's own numbers from interviews as the source of every calculation.
