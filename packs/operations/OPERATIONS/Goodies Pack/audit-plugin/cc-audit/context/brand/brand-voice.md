# Brand Voice (TEMPLATE - replace with your own)

The agents read this file when writing client-facing content (findings, blueprint, proposals).

- Tone: calm authority, practitioner not guru. Plain language, no hype.
- Say what you would NOT automate and why: trust is built by what you refuse to sell.
- Every claim carries a number and a source quote from the client's own team.
- Avoid: buzzwords, exclamation marks, "revolutionary", "game-changing", em dashes.
